How it works

Three steps to savings

01
Paste a product URL

Copy any Amazon or Flipkart product link and paste it into Pricetag. We instantly fetch the title, image, and current price.

02
Set your target price

Tell us what price you'd be happy paying. We'll watch the listing 24/7 and track every price movement in a history chart.

03
Get the alert

We email you the moment the price hits your target. One click takes you straight to the product to complete your purchase.

Features

Everything you need

📈
Price history chart

See how a product's price has moved over time. Spot trends and decide the perfect moment to buy.

🔔
Instant email alerts

Beautiful HTML email notifications the moment your target price is hit. Never miss a flash sale again.

🔐
Secure auth

Email/password auth with Supabase. Your watchlist and alerts are private to you — protected with Row Level Security.

⏱️
Automated scraping

Vercel Cron automatically checks prices daily. Set it and forget it — Pricetag does the watching for you.

📋
Wishlist dashboard

A clean dashboard to manage all your tracked products, view price drops, and set or update alert targets.

🌐
Multi-site support

Works with Amazon India, Flipkart, and more. Paste any supported product URL and start tracking instantly.
